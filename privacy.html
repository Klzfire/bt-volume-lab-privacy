BT Volume Lab — Privacy policy
Effective date: November 2, 2026

Developer and contact
BT Volume Lab is a free app published by klzfire. For privacy or support questions, contact cleanzfire@gmail.com.

Data stays on your device
BT Volume Lab does not send personal data to the developer or to a server. It has no advertising, analytics, tracking, account system or automatic crash reporting. The Android app does not request Internet permission.

Local data used for volume control
With your Shizuku or ADB authorization, the app reads the active Bluetooth headphone device identifier, media volume, audio routing and audio mode. It uses this information to target your connected headphones, show the current phone volume and avoid changing volume during calls. It does not read or record microphone audio, music contents, messages, contacts or location.

The last requested volume percentage is stored locally for each headphone device identifier. The identifier is used as the profile key; only its last characters are shown on the main screen. The privileged helper stores profiles in /data/local/tmp/bt-volume-lab-profiles.properties. They are not uploaded or applied automatically when headphones reconnect.

Permissions and Shizuku
Shizuku is a separate app. After you explicitly authorize BT Volume Lab, its helper runs with Android shell privileges to call system audio services. You can revoke access in Shizuku or stop Shizuku at any time. Shizuku has its own privacy practices; see https://shizuku.rikka.app/ for its documentation.

Retention and deletion
Profiles remain on the device until that local file is deleted or the device is reset. Because the file is owned by the Android shell user, uninstalling BT Volume Lab or clearing its app storage may leave it in place. To remove saved profiles with an authorized ADB connection, run:
adb shell rm /data/local/tmp/bt-volume-lab-profiles.properties
This removes saved levels. The app can create a new profile file after a later volume command. There is no developer-held account or remote profile to delete.

External links and support
Opening a website or email link uses your browser or email app and is subject to that service's privacy practices. If you email support, the developer receives the information you choose to include and uses it to answer your request. Avoid sending headphone identifiers or diagnostic logs unless needed for your question. Ask by email if you want support correspondence deleted.

Developer diagnostics
Optional computer-side diagnostic scripts are separate from the Android app's normal operation. They can save audio state and device identifiers on your computer. They do not upload those reports automatically.

Changes
This policy will be updated when the app's data practices change. The effective date above identifies this version.
